Factors Affecting Car Prices

Understanding these key factors can help you get a better idea of your car's market value.

Age & Year

Newer cars generally have higher market values, with prices depreciating significantly within the first few years.

Mileage

Higher mileage typically results in lower market value due to expected wear and tear on the vehicle.

Brand & Model

Popular brands and models often command higher resale values due to their reputation and demand.

Fuel Type

Different fuel types can affect pricing based on current market demand and fuel efficiency concerns.
