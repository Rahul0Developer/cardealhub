Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about buying, selling, and using Car Deal Hub.

Car Deal Hub is a comprehensive online platform that connects car buyers and sellers. We provide tools for price prediction, car listings, and market analysis to make the buying and selling process transparent and efficient. Our platform helps users make informed decisions with accurate car valuations based on market data.

Yes, Car Deal Hub is available across all major cities and regions in India. Our platform allows you to search for cars by location, making it easy to find vehicles in your area. We're continuously expanding our coverage to include more regions and provide better service to all users.

Our price predictions use advanced machine learning algorithms trained on extensive historical car sales data. The predictions are typically within 5-10% of actual market prices. Factors like car condition, service history, and local market variations can influence the final price. We continuously update our models to improve accuracy.

To contact a seller, simply click the "Contact Seller" button on any car listing. You'll be able to send a message directly through our platform. If you're logged in, your contact information will be automatically included, or you can choose to enter it manually. The seller will receive your inquiry and respond via email or phone as per their preference.

While Car Deal Hub doesn't directly provide vehicle history reports, we recommend asking the seller for service records and considering a third-party service like CarInfo or VAHAN for a comprehensive vehicle history check. This report can show past accidents, service history, ownership changes, and other vital information before making a purchase decision.

Yes, we highly recommend getting a pre-purchase inspection from a qualified mechanic before finalizing any used car purchase. Even cars that look perfect can have hidden mechanical issues that only a professional can identify. The cost of an inspection (typically ₹1,000-₹3,000) is a small price to pay compared to potential repair costs down the road.

Listing your car is simple. Click on "List Your Car" in the navigation menu, create an account if you don't already have one, and fill out the listing form with details about your vehicle. Include clear photos from multiple angles, a detailed description, and your asking price. Once submitted, our team will review the listing, and it should be live within 24 hours.

Selling time varies based on factors like car condition, price, demand for the model, and location. Popular models priced competitively typically sell within 2-3 weeks. Luxury vehicles or uncommon models may take longer. Ensure your listing has quality photos, a detailed description, and a competitive price based on our prediction tool to maximize your chances of a quick sale.

To sell your car legally, you'll need:

  • Original Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Valid insurance policy
  • PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate
  • Service history and maintenance records
  • Form 28, 29, 30 (available at your local RTO)
  • Bill of sale/receipt
  • NOC (No Objection Certificate) if the vehicle is registered in a different state

Creating an account is easy. Click the "Register" button in the top navigation, enter your email address, choose a username and password, and complete the registration form. You'll receive a verification email to confirm your account. Once verified, you can log in and access all features, including saving favorite cars, listing your own vehicles, and tracking your price predictions.

If you've forgotten your password, click the "Login" button, then select the "Forgot Password" link below the login form. Enter the email address associated with your account, and we'll send you a password reset link. Click the link in the email and follow the instructions to create a new password. For security reasons, the reset link expires after 24 hours.

Yes, you can delete your account at any time. Go to your account settings, scroll to the bottom, and click the "Delete Account" button. You'll be asked to confirm your decision. Please note that account deletion is permanent and removes all your data, including saved cars, listings, and prediction history. If you have active listings, they will be removed from the marketplace.

Yes, basic car listings are completely free on Car Deal Hub. You can list your vehicle with photos and all details at no cost. We also offer premium listing options for a small fee, which include highlighted placement, featured status on the homepage, and extended listing duration. These paid features can help your car sell faster by increasing its visibility.

For premium services, we accept all major credit and debit cards, UPI payments, net banking, and popular digital wallets. All transactions are processed securely through our payment gateway. For car transactions between buyers and sellers, we recommend secure payment methods like bank transfers or demand drafts, and we advise against cash payments for large amounts.

No, Car Deal Hub does not handle or process payments between buyers and sellers. We are a platform that connects parties, but all financial transactions for vehicle purchases occur directly between the buyer and seller. We recommend following secure payment practices and completing proper documentation during the sale process. Always meet in person to exchange documents and complete the transaction.
