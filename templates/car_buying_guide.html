Expert Car Buying Tips

Negotiate the Total Price, Not the Monthly Payment

Focus on the total cost of the car rather than monthly payments. Dealers may extend loan terms to lower monthly payments but end up charging more overall.

Research Market Value

Before negotiating, research what similar cars are selling for in your area. Knowing the market value gives you leverage in negotiations.

Get a Vehicle History Report

Always review the vehicle history report before purchasing a used car. It can reveal accidents, damage, and service history that may affect your decision.

Get a Pre-Purchase Inspection

For used cars, have a trusted mechanic inspect the vehicle before buying. A small inspection fee can save you from expensive repairs later.
