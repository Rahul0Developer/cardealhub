Best Practices for Selling Your Car

  • Thoroughly clean both the exterior and interior of your car
  • Fix minor issues such as scratches, dents, or broken lights
  • Consider a professional detailing service for better presentation
  • Replace worn wiper blades, floor mats, and other visible worn items
  • Ensure all maintenance is up to date (oil change, tire rotation, etc.)
  • Gather service records and maintenance history documents

Quality photos dramatically increase buyer interest. Follow these tips:

  • Photograph your car in good lighting, preferably during the "golden hour" (early morning or late afternoon)
  • Choose a clean, uncluttered background
  • Take photos from multiple angles (front, rear, sides, 3/4 views)
  • Include interior shots (dashboard, seats, trunk space)
  • Highlight special features (sunroof, navigation system, etc.)
  • Document any damage honestly to build trust with potential buyers
  • Aim for 10-15 high-quality images

Your ad description should be honest, detailed, and engaging:

  • Start with a compelling headline that includes year, make, model, and a standout feature
  • List all essential specifications (mileage, fuel type, transmission, etc.)
  • Mention recent maintenance, upgrades, or replacements
  • Be honest about the condition and any issues
  • Explain your reason for selling (builds trust)
  • Include details about the ownership history
  • Specify whether the price is negotiable
  • Provide contact information and preferred contact method
  • Respond promptly to all inquiries
  • Be prepared to answer detailed questions about the vehicle
  • Screen potential buyers before arranging meetings
  • Meet in public places for test drives and bring a friend if possible
  • Allow potential buyers to inspect the car thoroughly
  • Have all your paperwork ready to show (service records, registration, etc.)
  • Be willing to negotiate, but know your bottom line

Once you've found a buyer, complete these steps to finalize the sale:

  • Agree on the final price in writing
  • Accept secure payment methods (avoid personal checks)
  • Complete a bill of sale with buyer details, VIN, agreed price, and signatures
  • Transfer the title according to local regulations
  • Remove your license plates (if required in your region)
  • Provide all keys, manuals, and maintenance records
  • Cancel your insurance policy after the sale is complete
  • Notify your local transport authority of the ownership change
